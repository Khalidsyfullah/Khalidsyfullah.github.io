I am a PhD student in Computer Science at **Southern Illinois University Carbondale**, where I work as a Graduate Research Assistant. My research focuses on **vision–language models**, **AI reliability and security**, **multimodal learning**, **privacy-preserving distributed learning** (split and federated learning), **quantum machine learning** and **AI in healthcare**.

Before starting my PhD in Spring 2026, I was a Lecturer in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at the [Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST)](https://baust.edu.bd/cse/employees/md-khalid-syfullah/) from July 2023, where I also served as Departmental Program Coordinator and received the **Vice Chancellor's Teaching Excellence Award**. Earlier, I worked as an Android developer at A.K Apps Limited, building apps and Unity-based games. I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from **Khulna University of Engineering and Technology (KUET)** in 2023.

Outside research, I love travelling. I have explored most of Bangladesh and dream of walking on every continent.
